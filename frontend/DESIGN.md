# Flexibits — colorful app theatre

## Overview

Friendly, polished software marketing and hospital care console with a bright white foundation and bursts of candy color. A rounded humanist sans gives benefit statements an approachable confidence, while detailed interface imagery, floating icons and softly dimensional device scenes make the product tangible. Spacious chapters alternate with useful, text-rich cards; a charcoal variant brings the same playful language to dark product presentations.

### Essentials

- A product identity leads before the sales promise: center an icon-and-name lockup above a 40px/44px medium-weight benefit headline, with colorful oversized shapes cropped into both sides of the opening.
- Big chapter headings speak in paired tones, not tiny uppercase eyebrows: Effra 40px/44px muted lead-ins sit above 64px/64px dark statements, both at weight 500 and negative tracking.
- Actions feel like compact app controls: blue primary and pale-gray secondary pills are 44px high, have 24px corners and 31px horizontal padding, and sit 20px apart.
- Color arrives as theatrical intermissions: keep most of the landing page white or pale gray, reserve saturated yellow, violet and sky blue for discrete full-width proof or capability bands, and keep blue actions consistent throughout.
- The product floats rather than filling a browser template: stage real interfaces on isolated devices, layer small colorful symbols around them, and let selected media run beyond the 960px content width. When no device imagery exists, construct a legible interface fragment with floating capability icons.
- Feature explanations have a generous app-like shell: use two 460px cards separated by 40px, 20px corners and 50px padding; combine a colorful icon or device vignette, 40px title, 24px muted benefit and 18px explanatory copy.
- The closing is a miniature product theatre, not just another headline: place a centered icon, two-tone 44px identity, short summary, a 627px-wide rounded comparison or combination panel and paired actions before a black four-column footer. For one product, show two complementary modes or outcomes joined by a plus symbol.
